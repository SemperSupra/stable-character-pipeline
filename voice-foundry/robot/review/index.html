Audio Foundry: robot/computer voices

Original generic test phrases with three source voice presentations and three independent processing styles. Each playable sample is a public synthetic candidate, not a selected final character voice.

Qualification note: “Unspecified” means no gender presentation was assigned in this experiment. It is not a tested androgynous or asexual acoustic voice. Acoustic identity and processing are separate design axes. Human hearing-based quality assessment remains open.
