**Question:** After giving 13 original procedural head components a distinct EXR Object Index 3 (vs torso 1 and ground 2), do their *actual decoded full-resolution per-pixel masks* translate coherently with the source-known camera projection as rigid head position changes over three frames? Existing R060 jointly verifies depth/ID sampled geometry without a distinct portrait identity. This explicitly tests the semantic segmentation movement and source projection direction from actual RGB-independent EXR pixels.

**Fixed original study:** Blender Cycles 256×256, 65mm perspective, one original stylized bust scene, source-known head group world X offsets `[0,+0.36,-0.28]` at frames 0/1/2, no camera motion. Source head origin projected through Blender's native camera to pixel coordinates each frame. Original mesh/camera SHA and source pixel-center ray IndexOB/Z independently recorded and verified; independent official OpenEXR decodes full 256² object-index mask, extracts centroid/area, compares observed displacement to actual source projected horizontal displacement. A change in source semantics, another pass-ID, camera/pose/geometry SHA, missing head mask, nonfinite depth, stale or swapped EXR and unauthorized rights fails.

**Predeclared simple geometrical critic:** at least 64 stable source ray samples per frame, at least 8 native stable head-ID rays; real full-resolution head index mask area at least 500 pixels; 98% actual per-ray joint Z/object agreement within 0.05 uncalibrated source units, no Euclidean-vs-axial mixing. Source-projected and actual mask centroid horizontal displacement each must exceed 2 px and have the SAME sign, relative residual not exceed max(10 px, 45% of predicted absolute source dx) because visible silhouette centroid is **not** exactly the same as the mesh origin. Mask area ratios 0.65–1.35, actual changed mask fraction ≥0.005. This is a typed bounded directional consistency check, **not** optical flow or calibrated inter-frame feature correspondence.
