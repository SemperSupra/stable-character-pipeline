# AGENTS.md — Stable Character public source and candidate workcells

Read [canonical Way of Working](https://github.com/SemperSupra/engineering-governance-private/blob/main/WAY_OF_WORKING.md) and [agent delegation governance](https://github.com/SemperSupra/engineering-governance-private/blob/main/policies/agent-delegation.md) at their **authoritative locations**; do not copy them into another prompt or maintain a second policy.

This public repository owns **source, synthetic fixtures, public-safe test receipts and draft engineering PRs**. Current Audio Foundry owner: [issue #9](https://github.com/SemperSupra/stable-character-pipeline/issues/9). Spatial and voice work retain separate product-local scope and rights gates; selected final character identities/voice products remain under the private companion, not public runners.

Audio capability control R0 is **experimental**: [parallel execution workset / minimal actor baton](docs/architecture/AUDIO-CONTROL-PARALLEL-WORKSET.md), [cross-project source and prior-art audit](docs/architecture/AUDIO-CAPABILITY-CONTROL-R0.md), and existing #9 DLE. Do not treat a shared command schema as a deployed service, authority to capture/play audio, engine substitution, publishing permission, or license transfer.

Act through bounded, disjoint branches and direct GitHub DLE links; qualify source in public standard free-tier CI only with original, synthetic, rights-cleared material. No personal recordings, final identity-bearing voice artifacts, medical data, passwords, access tokens, private repos' value-bearing details, or paid/private build minutes. Do not merge, release, sign, enable live microphone/audio or expand public ingress without explicit owner authority.

For humans, automation and agents: capability discovery is source-adjacent documentation; CLI/tests/typed receipts should reference **one** accepted contract rather than divergent per-audience implementations. State-machine command acceptance is not proof of physical playback or recording. Native OS authorization, fail-closed Stop, target/lease control, output safety and real-device evidence remain independent gates.
