## A. Correct the earlier root-only impossibility overclaim

Earlier `sole_phase_root_feasibility_v1` treated a foot as supporting when at least five persistent shoe vertices were close to a *hypothetically lifted* floor in adjacent frames. This proximity criterion is **not sufficient to establish a planted foot**. It reported twelve alleged incompatible dual-support transitions per actor, with root-only translational lower-bound residual up to ~60mm. That arithmetic was valid *under its provisional support mask*, but using it to reject all root-only strategies was an unsupported causal inference.

The new independent witness recovers exact **72** original glTF poses from the SHA-pinned 35-joint research GLB. It uses both anklebone world-XZ motion **at most 8mm per sampled frame** and shoe-floor distance after *diagnostic-only* Y lift **at most 8mm at both frames**. It validates the older source correspondence input, original floor records, coordinates, source identity, original no-mutation flags, and each frame/side once. The 8mm thresholds are a diagnostic hypothesis, **not biomechanical contact truth**.
