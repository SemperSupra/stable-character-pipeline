# W4C native simulation bounds

Actual open-source MuJoCo Python wheel on public CPU loads original fictional hinge source MJCF. Numerically compares compiled joint bound and limited flag, probes out-of-bound qpos with native mj_forward and solver constraint observations; disabled limit flag must differ, tampered upper bound refused. Engine mechanics do not instantly clamp user-provided qpos, and do not establish real motor force, torque, safety, human anatomy or contact. No private source, paid runners or deployment.
