## Experimental hypothesis

Earlier AB and AC measured source- or moving-bottom-1%-selected shoe patches, not physically labeled heel/ball/toe contact. A source data scientist cannot conclude planted contact or heel/toe roll from a moving support centroid. This batch derives two disjoint, persistent rest-mesh material-ID cohorts per worn CC0 shoe side, oriented by actual rig evaluated foot.L/R head to toe1-1.L/R head projected onto Blender world XY at a fixed start pose. Determine bottom 25% mesh vertices (scene Z) once, then take rear and front 20% extrema of that cohort along the bone-forward axis. Restore IDs unchanged for all 12 subsequent native 24Hz frames. Preserve vendor footwear, source action and Blend bytes.

Two original fictional 163-bone MPFB rigs × two rig foot labels × start frames 24 and 48 = eight true native Blender shoe-side windows, with 24 observations per window (12 heel + 12 toe). Measure cohort XY speed, near-reference-floor pair counts and min-Z penetrations. Foot-to-toe bone direction is a *labeling proxy*; side rest-X partition F1 maps a prequalified high-dominance rig side but is not an anatomical foot scanner or force sensor. Never call the cohorts true heel/ball joints or accept stance without independent calibration. Lack of near-ground consecutive samples is UNKNOWN.
