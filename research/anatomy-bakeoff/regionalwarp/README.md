# R036 regionally deformable landmark fit — source-clean qualification

This independent, open source Go stdlib derivative is stacked on the already-verified R036 landmark/laterality contract PR #570, without changing its source or its authoritative DLE. The **algorithm is a bounded RBF nonrigid deformation field** in the already source→target-aligned frame. It requires at least eight separately sourced left/right and anterior/dorsal fiducial pairs from the parent contract, plus **two disjoint withheld independent landmarks** to assess generalization. It never discovers actual anatomy or fabricates real HRA or MPFB source landmarks.

Method: fixed-radius 3D isotropic Gaussian kernel `phi(r)=exp(-r²/(2σ²))`, default σ=.65 metres; ridge-regularized interpolation of three-dimensional residual displacement after the proper positive-determinant source-to-target affine frame. A tiny original stdlib pivoted Gaussian elimination solver computes per-marker displacement coefficients. Limits: <=128 input pairs, heldouts 2–64, source displacement cap .15m, source/target landmark SHA+frame, nonzero uncertainty, and analytic local Jacobian determinant sampled at all training/heldout points and selected pair midpoints. Local Jacobian ratio outside [.50,2.0] flags strain/inversion HOLD rather than acceptance. Heldout residuals must decrease by >0.01mm and training improve; otherwise report explicit HOLD. The field does not claim global invertibility from sampled Jacobians or medically meaningful elasticity, and is not itself a SOFA/FEBio tissue model.

A smooth deformation that fits eight genuine body-surface fiducials is not evidence that the real HRA lung is correctly inside ribs or pleura. A separate 3D signed-containment/collision test against verified torso skeletal/diaphragm surfaces remains mandatory. Other unqualified questions: HRA/MPFB vertex-to-surface transfer, left/right lung hilar connection, attachment of sternum/ribs, simulation inertia, histological material laws, subject phenotype mapping, and final photoreal video acceptance.

Only original synthetic fixtures are used in free public GitHub Actions CPU: a known mild proper anatomical correspondence, independently withheld source points, mirrored/swap negative controls, high deformation, lack of evidence, nonfinite inputs, adversarial heldout and strain checks. No HRA source binary, private named-person images, protected identity, paid cloud or private GHA minutes. This research layer should eventually consume real expert-reviewed HRA↔MPFB fiducials, but must not silently bridge missing anatomical facts. No merge/deploy/release and MVP HOLD unchanged.
