# R036 laterality, landmark and anatomical registration evidence contract

Original Go stdlib **source-only** companion to registered-source atlas work, stacked on existing R036 typed fidelity #562 without modifying its ownership. Any numerical point-cloud ICP comparison (including HRA→MPFB #560/#568) **must remain unqualified** until a separate manual or source-supported set of matched anatomical landmarks exists.

This contract does NOT pretend to infer human anatomy from a body mesh. It requires eight explicit, separately evidenced *source* and *target* fiducials: left/right clavicle, left/right iliac crest, sternal notch, T7 spinous process, head top and sacrum. Non-zero source and target uncertainty in metres and source record digests are required; exact atlas and character source SHAs, source and target coordinate-frame names and SI units are mandatory. Source↔target transform must be affine, proper (det>0), uniform-scaled (no shear) and nondegenerate. Anatomical left/right and anterior/dorsal vectors have to align under the proposed transform. At least one noncoplanar set is required; degenerate, mirrored, mislabeled, unsupported, forged/provenance-empty input is rejected.

Tests include a known proper scaled rotation, mirrored transform, swapped clavicular laterality, missing landmark, nonfinite/unphysical transform, duplicate source marker, coplanar set, arbitrary 4cm shift, evidence absence and confidence violation. A small numerical residual and complete inputs return **MECHANICAL_CORRESPONDENCE_ONLY_INDEPENDENT_ANATOMY_REVIEW_REQUIRED**. No amount of synthetic/declared test data makes a real organ medically correct; fiducial credentials and human/local qualified observation are separate authorization/quality gates.

Implementation intentionally **does not** fit actual HUMap or MakeHuman fiducials. Those 3D locations and their HRA phenotype/MPFB crosswalk are not yet independently acquired/qualified and must not be fabricated. Next required work is a source-backed fiducial picker or anatomical landmark donor with human-reviewed coordinates and uncertainty; only after that would the proper-rotation registration solver and nonrigid fit become eligible for anatomical acceptance. Source-only synthetic tests safe for public free GHA; no protected named character, source model binaries, private runner, merge/release or deployment.
