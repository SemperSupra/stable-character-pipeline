# Actual HuBMAP lung + native MPFB phenotype refit — R036 composition

**This is the first composed real-donor-anatomy/morph experiment** (not two independent proxies). Stacked solely on source-pinned, rights-gated HRA lung integration draft #552 at exact head `ce087fe7d9a6189e09400896dd31d789eebc3344`, and reuses its original upstream 163-bone MPFB/Blender fixture. It does not modify that owning branch.

The already SHA256-locked HuBMAP 2022 lung GLB (`e64351b72b0bc5289a4dc32ba2b402b9c974f9ec0b1972bf06b436864f917913`) is source geometry licensed CC BY 4.0. The 67 real anatomical submeshes are loaded in disposable runner scratch. From their source geometry, capture source vertex/topology SHA and each organ submesh centroid relative to the official `spine01` bone rest frame normalized by its length. Apply two actual `HumanObjectProperties` height/weight macro changes through MPFB `TargetService.reapply_macro_details()` and `HumanService.refit()`. Observe uncorrected mismatch, then rebind submesh locations and scale to the new bone frame, maintaining original source polygon topology. Enforce tiny numerical *synthetic binding invariant*, check real body and source organ both animate, and reopen saved Blender in a fresh process.

A bone-relative *rigid* mapping does not solve anatomical placement inside the body, lung inflation, thoracic deformation or mechanical collisions. The initial HRA target placement is intentionally a 0.30m artistic preview relative to a spine bone; its 3D reference orientation and anatomy have not been registered to an actual individual's thoracic landmarks. An exact bone-relative coordinate is not medical truth. Real organ location/collision and physics must be independently accepted separately.
