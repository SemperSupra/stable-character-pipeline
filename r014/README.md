# R014 adult anatomical coverage (research; HOLD)

This separate lane records the owner directive to **accurately model adult human anatomy in its entirety**, including external sexual/reproductive features, in a neutral anatomical and video-production context.

Neither current MPFB nor MHR baseline is known to provide accepted anatomy: the validator explicitly emits `HOLD_UNVERIFIED` for both.

MakeHuman historically moved genital proxy meshes out of its core distribution and into user-contributed downloads; these may have **different licenses**, separate from core CC0 graphics. Never silently download, promote or relicense contributed proxies. Refer to https://static.makehumancommunity.org/oldsite/faq/what_happened_to_the_special_assets_genitalia.html .

The JSON contract is a coverage checklist and a claim-falsification mechanism, not anatomy meshes. A manifest-only pass must never be promoted to visual/video or medical-accuracy qualification. Per-feature topology and anatomical review remain future work. Neutral adult reference assets only; no inferred anatomy for identifiable people.
